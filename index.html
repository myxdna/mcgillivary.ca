GTM AI

My day job is turning go-to-market work into systems: outbound pipelines that find and score accounts from public signals, models that read sales calls at scale, and AI tools that help reps practise and prepare. I build them end to end: front end, back end, data, and the AWS infrastructure that deploys them, written as code in CDK with TypeScript. I publish a build log of each one — the tools, the prompts, the architecture and what it costs — so other teams can take them.

Campsite Sniper

Ontario Parks campsites sell out months ahead. This bot watches for cancellations on the dates and sites I want, alerts me the moment one opens, and can carry the booking through checkout. Runs 24/7 on a Raspberry Pi.

  • Python
  • Automation
  • Raspberry Pi

MTG Proxy Printer

Prints playtest card sheets on a home inkjet with accurate colour. A scanner-based calibration loop measures printed swatches and builds a correction profile so cards match their on-screen art.

  • Colour science
  • Image processing

Local LLM Offload

About

I'm based in Ottawa, Canada, and lead GTM AI in the tech industry, where I get to build a lot of cool stuff. Most of my side projects start with a small annoyance — a campsite that's always booked, a kid who can't decide on lunch, a card game I want to play with friends in other cities — and end with something running on hardware at home.

I like shipping working software quickly, then making it reliable.
